Cannon Blast: Knock Down Cans - Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026

This policy explains how Antirot Arcade ("we", "us") handles information when you play Cannon Blast: Knock Down Cans (the "Game") on iOS. The Game has no accounts and never asks for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

1. Information we and our partners collect

  • Device and advertising identifiers: the identifier for vendors (IDFV), a Firebase installation ID and, only if you allow it in the iOS "Allow Tracking" prompt, the advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • Gameplay and usage data: levels played and completed, boosters used, rewards claimed, session length, ads viewed and clicked.
  • Device information: device model, OS version, language, time zone, app version, approximate country/region derived from your IP address.
  • Diagnostics: crash reports and performance data.
  • Purchases (only if in-app purchases are offered): product bought and price. Payments are processed by Apple; we never receive your payment details.

Your game progress (level, coins, boosters) is stored only on your device.

2. How we use it

  • To run the Game, fix crashes and improve levels and difficulty (analytics, diagnostics, remote configuration).
  • To show ads that keep the Game free, measure ad performance and, where you consented, personalise ads.

We do not sell your personal information for money. Sharing identifiers with advertising partners to show personalised ads may count as "sharing" or "targeted advertising" under some laws; you can opt out as described below.

4. Your choices

  • Tracking: iOS asks whether the Game may track you. You can change this any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Personalised ads (EEA, UK, Switzerland): you are asked for consent on first launch and can change it from the privacy option in the Game's settings.
  • Notifications: you can turn them off in iOS Settings.
  • Deleting data: uninstalling the Game deletes progress stored on the device. To ask us to delete data held by our partners about your device, email us with the request (see Contact).

5. Children

The Game is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Data retention and security

Analytics and diagnostics data are kept by our partners for the period set in their services (for example up to 14 months for Google Analytics). Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example GDPR in the EU/UK, CCPA/CPRA in California, PDPD Decree 13/2023 in Vietnam) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your data. Contact us to exercise them.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change and significant changes will be announced in the Game or on the store page.
