Antirot Arcade - Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026

This policy explains how Antirot Arcade ("we", "us") handles information when you play any of our games on iOS or Android (each a "Game"). It applies to every game listed below and to any game whose store page links to this page.

Games covered

  • Cannon Blast: Knock Down Cans
  • Sand Party

Our games have no accounts and never ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

1. Information we and our partners collect

  • Device and advertising identifiers: the identifier for vendors (IDFV) on iOS, a Firebase installation ID and, only where you allow it (the iOS "Allow Tracking" prompt, or your Android ad settings), the advertising identifier (IDFA / Google Advertising ID).
  • Gameplay and usage data: levels played and completed, items or boosters used, rewards claimed, session length, ads viewed and clicked.
  • Device information: device model, OS version, language, time zone, app version, approximate country/region derived from your IP address.
  • Diagnostics: crash reports and performance data.
  • Purchases (only in games that offer in-app purchases): product bought and price. Payments are processed by Apple or Google; we never receive your payment details.

Your game progress is stored only on your device.

2. How we use it

  • To run the Games, fix crashes and improve levels and difficulty (analytics, diagnostics, remote configuration).
  • To show ads that keep the Games free, measure ad performance and, where you consented, personalise ads.

We do not sell your personal information for money. Sharing identifiers with advertising partners to show personalised ads may count as "sharing" or "targeted advertising" under some laws; you can opt out as described below.

4. Your choices

  • Tracking (iOS): iOS asks whether a Game may track you. You can change this any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Advertising ID (Android): you can reset or delete it in Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • Personalised ads (EEA, UK, Switzerland): you are asked for consent on first launch and can change it from the privacy option in each Game's settings.
  • Notifications: you can turn them off in your device settings.
  • Deleting data: uninstalling a Game deletes progress stored on the device. To ask us to delete data held by our partners about your device, email us with the request and the name of the Game (see Contact).

5. Children

Our Games are general-audience games and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Data retention and security

Analytics and diagnostics data are kept by our partners for the period set in their services (for example up to 14 months for Google Analytics). Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example GDPR in the EU/UK, CCPA/CPRA in California, PDPD Decree 13/2023 in Vietnam) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your data. Contact us to exercise them.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change and significant changes will be announced in the Games or on their store pages. If a Game uses a service not listed above, it is added here before that version is released.
